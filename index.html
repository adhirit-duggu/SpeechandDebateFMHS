What We Do

🗣️ Debate Events

  • Public Forum: Team-based current events debate.
  • Lincoln-Douglas: 1v1 value & ethical debate.
  • Congressional Debate: Mock legislative debate on real bills.

🎭 Speech Events

  • Interpretation: Dramatic, Humorous, & Duo storytelling.
  • Oratory & Extemp: Original speeches & quick-prep commentary on world events.
