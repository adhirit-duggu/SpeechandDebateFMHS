Legislative Debate

Mock Congress

Congressional Debate

Ever watched Congress on TV and thought, "I could do a much better job"? Here’s your chance to prove it. In Congress, you simulate actual lawmakers by debating fake bills, giving short speeches, and grilling other students with questions. It’s loud, fast, and awesome for building real leadership, quick thinking, and confidence in front of a crowd.

Format: Individual / Chamber Assembly | Prep: Docket-based

Debate Events

2v2 Partner Debate

Public Forum Debate (PF)

If you love arguing about what’s happening in the news right now, this is your spot. PF is super practical because you partner up with a friend to debate real-world issues that actually affect our lives today. You’ll learn how to think fast during cross-examination, back up your points with solid evidence, and explain big global topics in a way that anyone can understand.

Format: 2 vs 2 | Topics: Monthly Current Events
1v1 Value Debate

Lincoln-Douglas Debate (LD)

If you’re the type of person who constantly asks "why" or loves a good debate about ethics, give LD a try. It’s a 1v1 debate event focused heavily on philosophy, morals, and values. Instead of just picking apart statistics, you get to argue about what’s right versus wrong and build air-tight logical arguments to defend what you believe in.

Format: 1 vs 1 | Topics: Bi-monthly Philosophical/Policy
2v2 Policy Debate

Policy Debate (CX)

If you want a intense, deep-dive challenge where you become an absolute expert on a topic, Policy is for you. You and a partner spend the year researching massive national problems and proposing specific solutions. It takes a ton of dedication and evidence, but nothing beats the rush of out-maneuvering another team with sheer research and strategy.

Format: 2 vs 2 | Topics: Year-long Resolution

Public Speaking Events

Limited Prep

Extemporaneous Speaking

If you're secretly a news junkie who likes a high-energy challenge, Extemp is ridiculously rewarding. You get handed a current events question on the spot, get 30 minutes to prep, and then deliver a full 7-minute speech with no notes. It sounds terrifying at first, but once you get the hang of it, you feel like an absolute genius on stage.

Format: 30-Min Prep, 7-Min Speech | Focus: Global/US News
Original Persuasion

Original Oratory (OO)

Got a topic or message you’re genuinely passionate about that you wish more people listened to? OO lets you write and deliver your own 10-minute persuasive speech from scratch. Whether you want to share a personal experience, call out a societal issue, or inspire people to change, this is your platform to make an audience actually care.

Format: Memorized 10-Min Speech | Focus: Persuasive Topic
Informative Speaking

Informative Speaking (INFO)

If you love diving down random Wikipedia rabbit holes and telling your friends weird facts, you'll thrive here. Info lets you pick literally any topic you find fascinating—from artificial intelligence to the psychology of sleep—and turn it into an engaging, creative presentation. You can even use visual aids to bring your topic to life!

Format: Memorized 10-Min Speech | Visual Props: Optional
Limited Prep

Impromptu Speaking

If you’re great at coming up with clever responses on the spot, Impromptu is pure fun. You draw a random prompt (like a quote, an object, or a single word), take a quick minute to organize your thoughts, and start speaking. There's zero stress about spending weeks writing or researching beforehand—it’s all about fast thinking and personality.

Format: 7-Min Total (Prep + Speech) | Focus: On-the-Spot Prompts

Interpretation Events

Acting / Drama

Dramatic Interp (DI)

If you love theater, deep acting, and pushing yourself emotionally, DI lets you take center stage. You choose a powerful script or book excerpt and act out a intense story all by yourself, without any props or costumes. It’s raw, emotional, and lets you bring incredible characters to life using just your voice and expressions.

Format: Solo Performance | Time Limit: 10 Minutes
Acting / Comedy

Humorous Interp (HI)

If you love making people laugh and doing funny voices, HI is basically high school stand-up combined with acting. You pick a funny play or book and play all the different characters yourself. It takes a ton of energy and physical comedy, but getting a whole room of judges and competitors to crack up is the best feeling ever.

Format: Solo Performance | Time Limit: 10 Minutes
Partner Performance

Duo Interpretation (DUO)

Want to act, but don't want to do it alone? Duo pairs you up with a partner to perform a 10-minute scene together. The twist? You aren't allowed to look at or touch each other during the scene, which forces you to get super creative with your timing, eye contact, and chemistry to pull off an unforgettable performance.

Format: 2 Performers | Time Limit: 10 Minutes
Literature Reading

Program Oral Interp (POI)

If you like mixing different genres together to tell a unique story, POI gives you total creative control. You weave together snippets of poetry, play scripts, and news articles centered around one single theme. You hold a black binder as your performance piece, using it to transition between totally different styles and emotions.

Format: Solo Performance | Time Limit: 10 Minutes
